Add playlist

Finds playlists, and videos over 20 minutes that have chapters.

Your playlists

Playlists must be Public or Unlisted. Private playlists, and videos whose owners block embedding, will not play.

API key and chapters

With a key saved, next and previous jump between chapters when a video has them. After the last chapter, next goes to the next video. The key is stored only on this phone.
